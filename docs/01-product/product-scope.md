# Product Scope

**Status:** accepted

## In scope

### Platform administration
Tenant lifecycle, platform administration, placement metadata, commercial customer metadata, global catalog/configuration, and controlled cross-tenant operations.

### Identity and access
Authentication, MFA, sessions, external identity integration, memberships, roles, permissions, custom tenant roles, machine/API principals, and privileged access controls.

### Monitoring
Monitoring-source integration, resource/device normalization, current operational state, metric definitions and samples, health, problems, synchronization, and monitoring dashboards.

### Alerting and notification
Alert lifecycle, notification policies, delivery channels, escalation, push, real-time delivery, delivery history, and integration-triggered notifications.

### ITSM
Incidents/tickets, comments, changes/RFCs, approvals, tasks, service/SLA modeling, maintenance windows, knowledge, ITSM integrations, and ChatOps-facing operational workflows.

### Automation
Scripts, parameters, controlled execution, schedules, workflows, execution history, approval/policy hooks, and asynchronous execution.

### Infrastructure
Assets, relationships/topology, firewall/SSL/backup state, Kubernetes visibility, capacity, discovery, configuration drift, and patch governance.

### AIOps
Anomaly detection, prediction, correlation, derived findings, confidence/traceability metadata, and configurable analysis strategies.

### FinOps and commercial capability
Infrastructure cost entries, budgets and optimizations, plus customer contracts, plans, billing cycles and payment-provider integration where modeled by the accepted domain boundaries.

### Reporting and experience
Reports, scheduled delivery, executive projections, public status projections, NOC/TV views, client-facing portals, and white-label presentation.

### Integrations and extensibility
API keys, webhooks, external connectors, marketplace catalog/installations, import/export, and governed extension points.

### Governance
Audit, compliance controls/evidence, LGPD/data-rights workflows, data retention, security audit capabilities, and governed data transfer.

## Scope evolution

A capability enters implementation scope only when its requirements, owner, trust boundary, data ownership, contracts, failure behavior, and acceptance criteria are defined. Being named in product scope does not mean all sub-capabilities ship in the first release.
